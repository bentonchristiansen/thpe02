On a rainy afternoon, Daniel wandered into the old library at the edge of town.
The air smelled of dust and paper. Rows of tall shelves stretched into the shadows.
He wasn’t looking for anything special, but something seemed to call to him.
A small blue book sat alone on a wooden table.
Its cover was faded, and the title was almost gone.
Daniel opened the book.
The first page said, "To the one who seeks, the door will open."
He laughed, but the lights flickered.
A cold wind swept through the room, and one shelf began to move.
Behind it was a narrow door.
Daniel hesitated, heart racing.
He stepped closer, holding the book like a shield.
The door creaked open, revealing a staircase leading down.
From below came a faint light-and a whisper that said his name.
Daniel took a deep breath and descended.
The door closed behind him, and the library fell silent once more.
